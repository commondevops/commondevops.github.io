FAQ

What size teams do you work with?

We work with teams of all sizes — from early-stage startups building their first CI/CD pipeline to enterprise engineering orgs standardizing across multiple platforms.

Do you embed with our team or work separately?

We embed. Our engagements are hands-on — we work inside your repos, infrastructure, and tooling alongside your engineers rather than delivering recommendations from the outside.

What cloud platforms do you support?

We work across AWS, GCP, and Azure, as well as hybrid and on-prem environments. Our approach is platform-agnostic and toolchain-flexible.

How long does a typical engagement take?

Engagements typically range from 6 to 16 weeks depending on scope. We start with an assessment phase to give you a realistic timeline before any commitment.

What does success look like at the end of an engagement?

Your team owns the system, understands it, and can operate it without us. We measure success by adoption, reduced incident rate, and faster delivery — not just deliverables shipped.
